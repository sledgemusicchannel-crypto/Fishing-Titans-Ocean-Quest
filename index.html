FULL DESCRIPTION

Welcome to Fishing Titans: Ocean Quest by Apex Mobility System! Drive your fishing boat across surface waves or jump directly into the sea to swim underwater using responsive on-screen D-Pad controls. Catch fish, open Mario-style '?' mystery crates, and battle deep-sea hazards across 5 ocean biomes to rescue Princess Marina! FEATURES: • Responsive On-Screen Touch D-Pad + Full Keyboard Controls (WASD / Arrows + Space / E / F). • Dual Boat & Swim Gameplay: Drive your boat & cast hook OR jump into the water to swim in 4 directions! • Mario '?' Mystery Crates: Fever Frenzy, Time Freezes, and Coin Showers. • Synthesized Chiptune Web Audio: Retro NES sounds and BGM loop with instant sound toggle. • Angler Growth Rank & Shop: Upgrade Reel Speed, Magnet Hook, Hydro Fins, Scuba Suits, and Boats! • Mobile Optimized: Built by Apex Mobility System for Android & Web.
